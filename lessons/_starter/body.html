Find the volume under \(z = x + y\) over the square \([0,1]\times[0,1]\).

  1. Set it up
    \[\int_0^1\!\!\int_0^1 (x + y)\,dy\,dx\]
  2. Integrate inside-out

    The inner integral gives \(x + \tfrac12\). Integrate that from 0 to 1.

Reveal the answer
\(V = 1\)\(\tfrac12 + \tfrac12 = 1\).
